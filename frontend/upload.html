What happens after upload

Backend pipeline

1 · Extract text

The PDF text layer is read page by page, PowerPoint slide text and speaker notes are read via python-pptx, images use local OCR, and .txt/.md files are read directly.

2 · Chunk & embed

Content is split into passages and embedded into PostgreSQL/pgvector.

3 · Detect topics

Passages are grouped into topics that feed mastery tracking.

4 · Tutor ready

The tutor and quiz generator can cite this material with its source.

Sources are extracted, chunked and embedded locally, then stored in your own PostgreSQL + pgvector database so the tutor and assessments can cite them.
