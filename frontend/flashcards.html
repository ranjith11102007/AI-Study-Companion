Why active recall

Retrieving an answer from memory strengthens it far more than re-reading — every flip is a mini test.

Spaced repetition

Cards marked "review again" resurface sooner and "knew it" cards wait longer, following a forgetting curve.

Sourced answers

Generated cards link back to the paragraph they came from, so a hazy answer is one click from the source.
