What are the four necessary conditions for deadlock, and how does circular wait work?

done_all Verified against 3 uploads
neurology
StudyMate OS verified 100% Sourced

Deadlock can occur if and only if all four of the following conditions hold simultaneously in an operating system:

1
Mutual Exclusion

At least one resource must be held in a non-shareable mode (only one process can use it at a time).

2
Hold and Wait

A process must currently hold at least one resource and simultaneously request additional resources held by other processes.

3
No Preemption

Resources cannot be forcibly preempted; they can only be released voluntarily after that process finishes its task.

4
Circular Wait

A closed chain of processes exists such that each process holds at least one resource needed by the next process in the cycle (e.g., P₀ waits for P₁, and P₁ waits for P₀).

lightbulb

Core Takeaway: Breaking even one of these four Coffman conditions guarantees deadlock cannot occur.
